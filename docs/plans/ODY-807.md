## Gaps found in the code

1. **Carried comments don't say where they came from.** `carryComments` creates plain copies, so round 2 can't show "carried over from round 1", and each copy's `createdAt` is the copy's date. A schema change is needed (Task 1).
2. **Highlights don't follow typing.** `anchorDecorations` redraws from fixed offsets on every transaction. Typing earlier in a commented block shifts the highlight onto the wrong letters until the comments are found again, and the lesson view only finds them again from `blocksV2` state, which is the last _loaded_ copy. That's the ticket's "two sources of truth".
3. **Resolve is reviewer-only.** `resolveComment` refuses authors, and only open reviews can change. Any creator action needs a new server action (decision A).
4. **One review only.** `getReviewForDroplet` returns a single review (`pageSize: 1`). With two reviewers, the creator sees one of them. ODY-798's open question 3 (one reviewer or several) is still open.
5. **ODY-847** ("latest review" sorts by `round`) doesn't affect 807. The creator view reads reviews on the draft, and a new `[EDIT]` draft starts with none. Note it in the PR; don't fix it here.

## Decisions

- **A. Can a creator mark a comment addressed? (decided: see the top)**
  - **Recommended: yes, "Mark addressed", which doesn't resolve.**
    - New `addressedAt` field on `review-comment`, set by a new author-only action.
    - The comment stays open, carries into the next round with its mark, and the reviewer's round shows "Marked addressed". Only the reviewer resolves.
  - **Alternative: read-only for the creator.** Less to build (no schema field, no new action), but the reviewer re-reads every comment each round.
- **B. Where do highlights show? (decided: see the top)**
  - **Recommended: in the BlockNote editor itself**, as the prototype and the ticket's live-content note both assume. `anchorsPlugin` already draws there.
  - **Alternative: a read-only preview next to the editor.** Simpler, but the creator edits in one place and reads feedback in another.
- **C. The panel, since ODY-805 isn't built. (decided: see the top)**

  - **Recommended:** build a small read-only "Reviewer feedback" panel now, matching the prototype's rail card, with:

    - the summary note;
    - confirmed scores per lesson and Overall;
    - the comment list in reading order;
    - the Outdated group.

    ODY-805 later hosts it inside the Transcript panel. Agree the component boundary with Jaylen first.

  - **Alternative:** build only the in-lesson part now (marks, tags, cards, the strip) and leave the panel to 805.

- **D. The Overview for a creator. (decided: see the top)**
  - **Recommended: a Feedback / Edit switch**, like the reviewer's bar.
    - **Feedback** is 806's read-only Overview with marks, section tags and cards, so it reuses that code as is.
    - **Edit** is today's editors.
    - The Overview's editors are separate TipTap editors and inputs, so drawing in each one is a lot of work for little gain.
  - The Overview opens on Feedback while there are open comments.
- **E. While the draft is back in review (round 2), the creator is frozen (ODY-810). (default)** They still see the round-1 feedback, read-only. No question needed.
- **F. Several reviewers. (default)** Show the latest sent review only, as `getReviewForDroplet` does today, and list it under Known gaps until ODY-798's question 3 is answered.

## Tasks

Each task is one commit with its tests. Branch from `origin/develop` (804 and 806 are both there).

### Task 1: where a carried comment came from (backend)

- **Schema:** `review-comment` gains `carriedFrom`, a manyToOne relation to `review-comment`. It also gains `addressedAt`, a datetime. Follow `schema-change-checklist`, regenerate types, and update `types/index.d.ts` (`ReviewComment`).
- **`carryComments`:** sets `carriedFrom` on each copy, and copies `addressedAt`.
- **`fetchComments`:**
  - populates `carriedFrom` with `createdAt`, plus its review's `round` and `sentAt`, so a copy shows its original date;
  - requests `addressedAt`.
- **Tests:** extend `backend/tests/api/review-workflow.test.js` (a copy points at its original; a reply's copy points at its original reply) and `testing/requests/reviews-read.test.ts`.

### Task 2: "Mark addressed"

- **Action:** `markAddressed(commentId, addressed)` in `lib/requests/reviews.ts`, via `withAuth([])`.
- **Who may call it:** an author of the droplet (`assertOwner` with `bypassRoles: []`), and only on a comment of a `sent` review for that droplet. No PENDING_AUTH allowlist entry. Read `docs/agent/server-action-auth.md` first.
- **Validation:** a Zod schema in `lib/validations/review.ts`.
- **Cache:** `revalidateTag(CACHE_TAGS.review(dropletId))`.
- **Tests:**
  - refusal cases: a non-author, a reviewer who isn't an author, an open review, a comment from another droplet;
  - one success case for each of setting and clearing.

### Task 3: highlights that follow typing (`lib/anchors/editor.ts`)

- **Mapping:** `anchorsPlugin` keeps a `DecorationSet` in plugin state, maps it through `tr.mapping` on every edit, and rebuilds it only when the anchors change (the meta `refreshAnchors` already sets). A highlight then stays on its words as the creator types around them.
- **Live re-resolve:** `BlockNoteEditor` gets an `onContent(document)` callback, fired from the editor's own change event and throttled to one animation frame. The lesson re-runs `resolveAnchor` against `blockNoteContent(editor.document)`, never `blocksV2`. A comment whose text is deleted moves to Outdated at once, not after the 1.5 s save.
- **Reviewer modes:** unchanged. Comment mode is read-only, so nothing is mapped.
- **Tests (`tests/lib/anchors/editor.test.ts`, real BlockNote):**
  - typing before a mark keeps it on its words;
  - deleting the marked words drops the decoration;
  - `refreshAnchors` rebuilds the set.

### Task 4: the creator's comments, loaded once

- **Hook:** `useSentFeedback(dropletId, enabled)` in `hooks/`. It reads `getReviewForDroplet` (which already hides open reviews from authors) and holds the review, comments and scores. It holds `markAddressed` too, applied optimistically with rollback, as in `useReviewComments`.
- **Provider:** `ReviewCommentsProvider` in `draft-layout-shell.tsx` loads the creator's feedback when `isAuthor && !reviewing`, so the sidebar and the page share one load.
- **Visibility:** no second check in the UI. The hook only says what the server returned.
- **Tests:** `testing/hooks/use-sent-feedback.test.ts`:
  - loads a sent review;
  - returns nothing for an open one (the server returns null);
  - Addressed rolls back on a refusal.

### Task 5: the creator's lesson editor (`lesson-renderer.tsx`)

> **As built (2026-10-29):** instead of a `mode` prop on `CommentLayer`, a separate read-only `FeedbackLayer` (`components/draft/review/feedback-layer.tsx`). The reviewer's layer is built around making comments (caret, bars, composer, keyboard stops), so a view mode would have touched most of its lines. `FeedbackLayer` reuses its `measureTags`, `measureBlocks` and `commentMarkAt`, and `CommentTag` and `CommentCard`; the card gains `focusOnOpen`, `meta` and `onAddress`. The strip is `FeedbackStrip`. An edited quote whose context is unchanged is still found (ODY-801's rule 3); only text edited away with its surroundings goes to Outdated.

- **When:** for an author with sent feedback, while editing (`isOwnLock`), or frozen read-only during the next round.
- **Marks:** drawn in the editable BlockNote editor through Task 3, in the sent style ("tint over a solid line"; amber marks only, as in UI example 1). Resolved comments are hidden, or shown with the strip's "Show resolved".
- **Tags:** in the right margin, as in 806, numbered by `numberComments`, opening the card.
- **`CommentLayer` gains a `mode: "compose" | "view"` prop.** In `view` mode there's no caret, no block bars, no composer and no "Comment with the keyboard" stops. Clicks and drags go to the editor.
- **Clicking a highlight** puts the caret where clicked, as editing needs, and also opens that comment's card beside the text without taking focus. Esc closes it. The tag opens the card and focuses it.
- **The card (`CommentCard`) in view mode:**
  - shows "From Nadia · round 1 · sent 2 days ago" and the body;
  - shows Mark addressed / Addressed ✓;
  - has no Edit, Delete or Resolve, and no "only you can see this".
- **The strip under the title (`CommentStrip`):** "4 comments from Nadia on this lesson · 1 outdated", with the Outdated group holding the original quote, struck through (`OutdatedComments`), and a link to the panel (Task 6).
- **Classic lessons:** the classic editor has no lasting block ids or decorations. Show the strip and the panel list only, with Outdated worked out from the saved text. Listed under Known gaps.
- **Tests (`tests/components/draft/lesson-renderer-feedback.test.tsx`):**
  - **the ticket's test:** a creator with a sent review sees its marks, and one with an open review (the server returns null) sees none;
  - **editing a commented sentence away** moves it to Outdated before any save (the ticket's second test);
  - a click on a highlight opens the card and still edits;
  - no caret or bars show.
