# ODY-947 — Lessons: Highlight mode in the Notes sidebar

Status: plan only, waiting for Jaylen's answers to the design questions below. No code has changed.

## 1. Summary

Today a highlight takes two steps: select text, then click a colour in the selection toolbar. Highlight mode lets a student switch that second step off. They turn the mode on in the lesson's Notes panel and pick a colour once. While it's on, finishing a selection highlights it straight away in that colour, and a small toolbar on the new highlight keeps Note, Explain and Remove (undo) one click away. The work is frontend only: highlights are still saved through the existing `createHighlight` / `updateHighlight` / `deleteHighlight` server actions, so there is no schema, backend or server action change.

## 2. Design questions

Each has a recommendation; the sub-tickets assume it. Changing an answer mostly changes ticket 4.

**Q1. When does a selection count as finished?**

- A. Per input: mouse and pen on `pointerup` (after a `pointerdown` inside the lesson); keyboard when Shift is released with a selection inside the lesson; touch handled separately (Q8). Precise, and a drag never saves half-way; needs a small new hook.
- B. Keep the 150 ms settle timer for everything. No new code, but a mouse pause mid-drag saves a partial highlight, and a later drag over it merges or stacks.
- C. Settle timer, but longer (600 ms). Fewer mid-drag saves, still some, and every highlight feels slow.

Recommendation: **A**. Details: read the selection one frame after `pointerup` so double- and triple-click selections are complete; ignore Ctrl/Cmd+A (no Shift release) so "select all" never highlights the whole page; ignore selections that start or end outside the layer. Screen reader browse-mode selection doesn't send key events, so it falls back to today's toolbar via the settle timer.

**Q2. What does the toolbar do while the mode is on?**

- A. Hidden while selecting; after the highlight saves, the existing on-highlight toolbar opens on it (recolour, Note, Explain, Remove). Focus stays in the lesson, Tab reaches the toolbar next. Keeps every action one click away and reuses tested code.
- B. Hidden entirely; actions only by clicking the highlight afterwards. Cleanest, but Note and Explain become two clicks and undo is hidden.
- C. A new mini toolbar (Undo, Note, Explain). Clearer labels, but a second toolbar to build, test and keep in sync.

Recommendation: **A**, with the Remove button's label reading "Undo highlight" for a just-made highlight (ties into Q7).

**Q3. Selecting text that is exactly an existing highlight, in the mode?**

- A. Open the recolour/remove toolbar as today; nothing is saved. Safe; one extra click to recolour.
- B. Recolour it to the mode's colour. Fast, but a re-select to add a note silently recolours.
- C. Toggle it off (remove). Matches a "highlighter pen" feel, but zero-click deletion is easy to trigger by accident, and it deletes notes' highlights.

Recommendation: **A**. The same fallback covers two related cases: a selection that would merge across saved highlights (Q7), and a selection inside a same-colour highlight, where today nothing would be saved (`highlight-layer.tsx:421`).

**Q4. Do the mode and colour persist?**

- A. Nothing persists; the mode is off on every page load. Simplest; students re-enable per lesson.
- B. Colour in `localStorage` (per browser); mode on/off in `sessionStorage` (stays on across lessons in this tab, off in a new tab). Pick once per study session, and a forgotten mode doesn't surprise a student days later.
- C. Both per account in Strapi (a user preference field). Follows the student across devices, but needs a schema change, a server action and auth review for a small preference.

Recommendation: **B**, in a new zustand store with `persist` (pattern: `stores/debug-toggle-store.ts`). The toolbar's colour becomes the same colour, so picking one in the toolbar also changes the mode's colour. No backend change.

**Q5. Where do the switch and colour picker live, and what happens when the panel closes or Mentor opens?**

- A. In the Notes panel's existing header row beside the close button (`droplet-lesson-wrapper.tsx:225`). No layout shift for NotesBar's positioned notes; always visible at the top of the panel.
- B. Under the "My Notes" heading inside NotesBar. Closer to notes, but pushes every note out of line with its text, and NotesBar loads lazily (the control would flash in).
- C. On the fixed toggle buttons (`highlight-dropdown.tsx`). Reachable with any panel, but not what the ticket asks and it crowds ODY-914's toggle grouping.

Recommendation: **A**. A `role="switch"` button ("Highlight mode") and a five-swatch radio group (`role="radiogroup"`, arrow keys). Closing the panel or opening Mentor leaves the mode on; the indicator (Q6) shows it. Hidden for authors and when highlighting is off (`canHighlight` false).

**Q6. How does the student see the mode is on?**

- A. A coloured dot on the Notes toggle button, and its label says "Highlight mode on, Yellow". Visible with any panel open or closed; cheap.
- B. Also tint the text selection with the mode's colour (`::selection` under a data attribute on the layer). The drag previews the highlight; no new element.
- C. A custom highlighter cursor over lesson text. Familiar from PDF tools, but custom cursors are inconsistent across browsers and invisible on touch.

Recommendation: **A + B**.

**Q7. Undo for accidental highlights?**

- A. The post-highlight toolbar's Remove ("Undo highlight") deletes what was just made. Selections that would merge (extend or delete saved highlights) don't auto-save; they open today's toolbar with the colour preselected. Undo is then always a plain delete and can't lose a merged highlight's note or position.
- B. As A, plus a toast with an Undo action. Discoverable and announced, but toasts stack quickly when highlighting a lot.
- C. Full undo including merges (restore the base, recreate removed ones). Most complete; roughly doubles ticket 4 and new ids break links to them.

Recommendation: **A**. Screen readers hear "Highlighted in Yellow. Tab for options." from a polite live region in the layer.

**Q8. Touch and phones?**

- A. Auto-highlight after the selection settles with no finger down (about 800 ms). Zero taps, but iOS and Android selection-handle drags send no touch events to the page, so a pause while adjusting a handle saves a partial highlight.
- B. In the mode, the touch toolbar leads with one large "Highlight" button in the mode's colour. One tap instead of a small swatch, never saves a half-adjusted selection.
- C. No mode on touch (switch hidden below `sm`). Honest, but the ticket asks for touch.

Recommendation: **B**, as its own small ticket. Note the Notes panel is a fixed 380 px wide even on phones (`LESSON_PANEL.width`); that's ODY-544/ODY-926 territory, not this ticket.

**Q9. The 'Move highlight here' flow?**

- A. Pause the mode while a highlight is being moved: the toolbar shows "Move highlight here" as today, and the indicator says paused. No accidental new highlight; no change to the move flow.
- B. Auto-move onto the finished selection. Zero-click, but a mis-drag moves a highlight with its note.
- C. Turning the mode on cancels a move in progress. Simple, but loses the student's place.

Recommendation: **A**.

**Q10. Copying text while the mode is on?**

- A. Nothing special; students turn the mode off to copy. Simplest; risk of unwanted highlights.
- B. Holding Alt/Option while selecting skips auto-highlight and shows today's toolbar. One modifier, mouse/keyboard only; needs a line in the help text.

Recommendation: **B** (a few lines in ticket 4).

## 3. Proposed sub-tickets

### ODY-947a — Shared highlight colour and mode store

The toolbar's colour and the new mode need one home that survives lesson changes. This adds a small persisted store and makes the selection toolbar use it, so the last colour picked is the one used next time.

Behaviour:

- Picking a colour in the toolbar is remembered across lessons and reloads (per browser).
- Highlight mode starts off and stays as set within the tab.
- With the mode off, nothing else changes.

+++ Agent notes

- Files: new `frontend/stores/highlight-mode-store.ts` (zustand `persist`: colour in `localStorage`, `on` in `sessionStorage` via `partialize`/two stores, validated with `isHighlightColor`); `highlight-layer.tsx:214` reads and writes it instead of `useState`.
- Hydration: read after mount (`persist` `skipHydration` or a mounted flag) so server and client render the same.
- Tests: `tests/stores/highlight-mode-store.test.ts` (default, persist, invalid stored colour falls back); one case in `generic-block-renderer.test.tsx` that a picked colour is preselected next time.
- Code comments 1-2 lines max.

### ODY-947c — Know when a selection is finished

A shared hook that calls back when the reader stops selecting, rather than when the selection pauses. Highlight mode uses it; review comments can later.

Behaviour:

- Mouse and pen: once, after the button is released, with the final selection (incl. double- and triple-click).
- Keyboard: once, when Shift is released after Shift+arrow / Shift+Home/End.
- Not for Ctrl/Cmd+A, not for touch, not for selections outside the container.
- Reports whether Alt/Option was held.

+++ Agent notes

### ODY-947d — Auto-highlight in the lesson while the mode is on

The core: with the mode on, finishing a selection saves it in the mode's colour, then opens the toolbar on the new highlight with Note, Explain and Undo.

Behaviour:

- Mode on, selection finished (ticket c): saved in the mode's colour, no toolbar step; the highlight toolbar then opens on it, not focused; "Highlighted in Yellow" is announced.
- The toolbar doesn't open mid-drag.
- Exact match of a saved highlight, a selection that would merge, a same-colour selection inside a highlight, Alt/Option held, or a screen reader selection: today's toolbar, colour preselected.
- While moving a highlight: mode paused, "Move highlight here" as today.
- "Undo highlight" deletes the highlights just made.
- Mode off: exactly today's behaviour (existing tests unchanged).

## 4. Tests

Unit tests (Jest + Testing Library) are listed per ticket above. In short: the store (persist, fallback); the control (switch, radiogroup, hidden for authors); the dropdown dot and label; `useSelectionEnd` (mouse, keyboard, ignored cases); and a new `highlight-layer.test.tsx` for auto-highlight and every fallback. Existing `generic-block-renderer.test.tsx` selection tests must pass unchanged, which proves the mode-off path.

Manual check on dev:

1. Mode off: select, colour, Note, Explain, Remove, Move highlight here all as before.
2. Turn the mode on in the Notes panel, pick Green; close the panel. The Notes button shows a green dot.
3. Drag-select slowly, pausing mid-drag: nothing saves until release; then a green highlight and the toolbar.
4. Double-click a word, triple-click a paragraph: each highlights once.
5. Shift+arrows from the keyboard: highlights on Shift release; Tab reaches the toolbar; NVDA/VoiceOver announces it.
6. Ctrl/Cmd+A: no highlight. Alt+drag: today's toolbar.
7. Select across two blocks, inside a callout, inside a code block and over LaTeX.
8. Select exactly a saved highlight: recolour/remove toolbar, nothing saved. Select across a highlight's edge: today's toolbar.
9. Undo removes just the new highlight. Note and Explain from the post toolbar work.
10. Open Mentor: mode stays on and still works. Next lesson: still on, same colour. New tab: off, same colour.
11. Phone (iOS Safari, Android Chrome): long-press, adjust handles, tap "Highlight".
12. Move a detached highlight while the mode is on: "Move highlight here" as today.

## 5. Out of scope and risks

Out of scope: Notes panel placement (ODY-926), Notes/Mentor toggle grouping (ODY-914), panel sizing on phones and the hover-only Help tooltip (ODY-544), per-account preferences in Strapi, undo for merges, review comments using the new hook.

Risks:

- Unwanted highlights while copying or reading with a mouse habit of selecting text. Mitigated by the session-only mode, the indicator, Alt to skip and Undo.
- Browser differences in when the selection is final after `pointerup` (Safari, CodeMirror's editors). Reading one frame later covers the known cases; check on dev.
- Five pushed branches rewrite the Notes panel `div` and the dropdown test. Ticket b should go after them or keep to the header row.
- Saving takes a round trip before the post toolbar appears (~200 ms). If that feels slow, show it on the temp highlight and disable Undo until saved.
